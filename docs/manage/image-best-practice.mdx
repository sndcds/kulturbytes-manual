A good image helps people see what your event or venue is about, even in a small preview.

## Choose a clear subject [#clear-subject]

* Use an image that fits the event or venue.
* Choose one clear main subject instead of many small details.
* Check that the image is sharp and bright enough.
* For event and venue photos, a landscape image is a useful starting point.

## Leave room around the subject [#cropping]

The same image may appear wide, square or small. Parts of the edges may be cut off. Keep faces and other important details away from the edges, and set the **focus point** on the main subject.

## Keep text separate [#text]

Avoid long text, dates and logos in the main photo. They are hard to read in a small preview and may be cut off. Enter the title, date and venue in the event fields. Use the poster field for a poster with text.

## Describe the image [#description]

Add a short **alt text** that describes the important content, for example: “Three musicians playing on the stage at the cultural centre.” This helps people who cannot see the image.

## Before uploading [#before-uploading]

Use an image you are allowed to publish. Add the photographer and any supplied credit or licence information. Indicate if the image was created or substantially changed with AI.

Read [Images](/manage/images) for file formats and size recommendations, and [Logos](/manage/logos) for logo variants.
