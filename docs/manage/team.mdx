A **team** groups people who work together on an organization's information. Each person uses their own user account.

## Work together [#work-together]

Agree on who looks after organization details, venues and events. What a person can view or edit depends on their permissions.

If you cannot open or change something, ask a person who manages your organization to check your access.

## Keep access up to date [#keep-access-current]

Review who still needs access when responsibilities change. Use your own account and keep your password to yourself.

Learn more about [organizations and user accounts](/manage/organization#organization-and-user-account).
