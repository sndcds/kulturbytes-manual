A **portal** presents a selection of events and information from kulturbytes. For example, it can show offers for a region or a particular audience.

## One place to keep information up to date [#shared-information]

Continue to maintain descriptions, venues and dates in kulturbytes. A portal shows the information selected for it. You do not need to enter the whole event again for each portal.

## Check your selection [#check-selection]

Think about who the portal is for and which offers belong there. Check the displayed events regularly and keep the original information up to date.

To correct an event, open it under [Events](/manage/events).
