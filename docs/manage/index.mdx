Do you organise events or look after a cultural venue? Use the **Dashboard**, the area for managing your offers, to enter and update your information.

## Getting started [#getting-started]

1. Read the [overview](/manage/overview) to see how organizations, venues and events fit together.
2. [Create your user account](/manage/register) and log in.
3. Open the [Dashboard](/manage/dashboard).
4. Create your [organization](/manage/organization) and [fill in its details](/manage/organization-edit).
5. Add a [venue and, if needed, a space](/manage/venue-and-space).
6. [Create an event](/manage/events) and add its description and dates.
