You can use **JPEG, PNG and WebP** images for events and venues. kulturbytes adjusts the display size automatically, so you usually only need to upload one version.

For logos, a **PNG with a transparent background** is useful. This lets the logo appear on different backgrounds without a white rectangle around it.

## Recommended image dimensions [#recommended-image-dimensions]

Images around **1600 × 900 pixels (16:9)** are a useful starting point. Pixels are the small dots that make up a digital image. Larger images and other shapes, such as square or portrait images, are also supported.

For a specific format, such as a poster or social media image, use the shape requested by that field.

## How images are displayed [#how-images-are-displayed]

The original image is kept. For display, kulturbytes makes it smaller and crops it if needed. This means the edges may not be visible in every view.

Set the **focus point** on the main subject so it stays visible when the image is cropped.

### Tips for choosing images [#tips-for-choosing-images]

Choose a clear main subject and leave some space around it. Avoid long text and small logos in the photo. See [Tips for good images](/manage/image-best-practice) for an illustrated example.

## Image details [#image-details]

Alongside the image, add:

* **Alt text:** a short description of the important content, for people who cannot see the image.
* **Photographer:** the name of the person who took the photo.
* **Copyright:** who holds the rights to the image.
* **License:** the supplied terms for using the image.
* **AI label:** whether the image was created or substantially changed with artificial intelligence.
* **Description:** additional context, also used as a caption on detail pages.

For logos and their variants, see [Logos](/manage/logos).
