With kulturbytes, you can make your events and cultural offers visible. Start with your organization, add venues and spaces, and then create your events.

## Organization [#organization]

Your **organization** is the starting point. It can be an association, a museum, an initiative or you as an individual organizer. Here you keep your contact details and manage your offers.

### Venues and spaces [#venues-and-spaces]

A **venue** is a place where events happen, such as a cultural centre. A **space** is a specific area within it, such as the main hall.

Create each venue and space once. You can then use them for many events without entering the address again.

### Events [#events]

An **event** describes an offer, such as a concert. Add a venue, a space if needed, and one or more dates.

For example, you can create a jazz evening at a cultural centre and select the main hall as the space.

### Partnerships [#partnerships]

Organizations can work together, for example as partners or sponsors.

### Users and teams [#users-and-teams]

Several people can help maintain the information. Each person uses their own account. [Teams](/manage/team) group people who work together.

### Portals [#portals]

A [portal](/manage/portal) shows selected events, for example for a region or audience. You continue to keep the information up to date in kulturbytes.
