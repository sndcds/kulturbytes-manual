A **venue** is a place where events happen, such as a museum, theatre or cultural centre.

## Open the venue overview [#venue-overview]

Choose **Venues** on [kulturbytes](https://kulturbytes.de/en). Open a venue that interests you to see more information. You can also follow the venue link from an event.

## Plan your visit [#plan-your-visit]

Depending on the information available, a venue page shows its address, position on the map, description, website and contact details.

Check which venue and space are listed for your event. A cultural centre may have several halls or stages. If you need information about access, look for accessibility details or ask the venue directly.

To find something happening at a particular venue, use its name in the [event search](/discover/search#place).
