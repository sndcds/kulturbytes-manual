Looking for a concert, an exhibition or something to do at the weekend? On [kulturbytes](https://kulturbytes.de/en), you can browse events and discover cultural venues. You do not need an account to search.

## Getting started [#getting-started]

1. Open [kulturbytes](https://kulturbytes.de/en) and choose **Events**.
2. Open **Search** to enter a search term or narrow down the results.
3. Choose a time period and, if needed, a town or venue.
4. Open an event to see its description, dates and information about attending.

The search looks different on a phone and on a large screen. On a phone, choose **Show results** to apply your selection and return to the results.

## What would you like help with? [#topics]

* [Search for events](/discover/search): use search terms, dates and other options.
* [Tips for your search](/discover/tips): find more results or make your selection more precise.
* [Discover places](/discover/places): find out more about cultural venues and where they are.

To publish your own events, go to [Manage](/manage).
